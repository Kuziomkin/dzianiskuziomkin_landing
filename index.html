About

Most data projects fail not because of bad technology — but because nobody properly defined what the business actually needed. That gap is where I work. I've spent 8 years embedded in enterprise data teams, bridging the space between business stakeholders and engineering — turning ambiguous goals into requirements precise enough to build from.

I've led analysis on large-scale data platform migrations to Databricks and Microsoft Fabric, designed governance frameworks for multi-team environments, and built process automation workflows that removed weeks of manual overhead. Industries have included financial services, retail, and public sector — always in environments where data quality and compliance weren't optional.

Outside consulting, I ship my own software products: iOS and Android apps with paying users across multiple stores. Building products keeps my analyst instincts honest — when you're both the analyst and the person who has to ship, vague requirements have immediate consequences.
