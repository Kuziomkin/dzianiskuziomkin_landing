About

I spent 8 years as a Business Intelligence Analyst in enterprise environments — working across fintech, payments, banking, and insurance. I'm currently a Lead BI Analyst at EPAM Systems, based in Málaga, Spain. Originally from Belarus.

I'm obsessed with the gap between analysis and action — why good insights don't get implemented, and how to close that gap through better requirements, better systems, and better tooling.

Outside of client work, I build software products from scratch. Not as a hobby — as a serious parallel track. I believe the best analysts of the next decade will be people who can both understand a business problem and ship a solution.
