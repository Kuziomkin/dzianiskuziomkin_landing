About

CBAP-certified specialist with 8 years of experience in high-stakes, data-heavy environments. My focus is upstream: I translate what the business actually needs into requirements, process logic, and governance workflows — before anyone opens a BI tool.

Outside my day job I build my own products — iOS apps and AI-powered tools. It keeps me sharp on the product side and honest about what "good requirements" actually means when you're the one who has to ship.
